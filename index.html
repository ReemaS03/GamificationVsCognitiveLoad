In 2023, a group of researchers from the Sano Centre for Computational Medicine in Poland conducted a study to investigate whether gamifying surveys increased completion rates. Their study involved 13 participants who completed two experiments:

  1. Baseline: A test where participants relaxed for three minutes.
  2. Stroop: A cognitively demanding test that required participants to name the ink color of a word, where the word itself was the name of a color (most often different color than the ink). For instance, the word RED, which reads as one color but has been printed in another.
Through both of these experiments, the biometric signals of the participants were captured using two wearable devices:

  • Empatica E4 wristband
  • Samsung Galaxy Watch4

Our project visualizes this data in an intuitive, interactive format. We aim to recreate the gamified aspect of the survey by having our users engage in the Stroop Test, as the original participants did. Hit Explore below to get started!
