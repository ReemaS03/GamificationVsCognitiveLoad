Welcome to the Stroop Test!

The Stroop test measures cognitive interference. You must identify the color of the displayed word, ignoring the word itself.

Instructions:

  • You will be shown 3 words in total, one after the other.
  • Press the key on your keyboard corresponding to the color of the word:
    • R - Red
    • G - Green
    • B - Blue
    • Y - Yellow
  • For each question you get right, King Triton moves closer to the finish line.
  • You may attempt each word a maximum of 2 times.
  • If you would like to end the test early, hit the Go to Plot button to explore the scatterplot.

Good luck!
