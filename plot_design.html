Does Simple Gamification Impact the Cognitive Burden of Filling in Surveys?

This project explores physiological signals recorded from three different wearable devices (Empatica E4, Samsung Galaxy Watch4, and Muse S EEG Headband) to analyze how cognitive load affects participants.

Empatica E4 Wristband

A research-grade wearable device that measures BVP, EDA, and skin temperature.

Samsung Galaxy Watch4

A commercial smartwatch that primarily measures Blood Volume Pulse (BVP).

Muse S EEG Headband

A headband designed to capture EEG brainwave activity.

Conclusion

Through interactive visualizations, we explore how different physiological signals respond to cognitive load conditions across multiple wearable devices.
